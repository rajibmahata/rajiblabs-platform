Flagship projects

HistoriaAI

In development

Most history education content for Indian students defaults to English or Hindi, leaving Bengali speaking students without a tutor that teaches in their own language.

HistoriaAI is a Bengali-first AI tutor that walks K-12 students through Indian and regional history in conversational Bengali, adapting to how each student is progressing.

Stack: LLM-based tutoring engine, ASP.NET Core, Azure

ReturnGuard AI

In development

Retailers selling across multiple channels struggle to catch fraudulent or abusive return patterns before they eat into margin.

ReturnGuard AI is a copilot that watches return activity across channels and flags patterns that look like fraud or abuse, so a human reviewer can decide fast.

Stack: .NET backend, Azure, rules plus ML scoring

LexVault

In development

Legal teams need to search and reason over large volumes of case documents without losing precision or missing context.

LexVault retrieves legal documents through a dual-pipeline design: one pipeline optimized for precise clause lookup, and one for broader contextual reasoning.

Stack: RAG pipeline, ASP.NET Core, Azure, vector search

InboxPilot

In development

Support and sales inboxes drown teams in email that needs to be triaged, tagged, and routed before anyone can act on it.

InboxPilot is a multi-tenant SaaS product that triages incoming email automatically. It started as a proposal for a single client and grew into its own product.

Stack: ASP.NET Core, EF Core, Docker Compose
