01 — About

Architecture thinking, AI-native delivery

PestFlow

Live

Pest control companies in India needed a way to onboard as a GST-registered business, send professional quotations to customers, and get paid — online or in cash — without stitching together spreadsheets and manual paperwork.

PestFlow is a multi-tenant platform built for pest control companies: it handles company onboarding with GST registration, generates and emails customer quotations, and supports both online and cash payment collection. It currently powers the live site for APCS Pest Control.

Stack: ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, Azure, multi-tenant architecture

R.M. Enterprise CMS

Live

R.M. Enterprise needed to manage a growing food product catalog, update pricing and descriptions, and keep their public site current — without depending on a developer for every small change.

A custom content management system built for R.M. Enterprise, giving their team direct control over the product catalog, listings, and site content that powers Fryyo Foods' public website.

Stack: ASP.NET Core, EF Core, SQL Server, Azure

In development — the AI lab

AI-native products built end to end under RajibLabs, from problem framing to working prototype.

HistoriaAI

In development

Most history education content for Indian students defaults to English or Hindi, leaving Bengali-speaking students without a tutor that teaches in their own language, at their own pace.

HistoriaAI is a Bengali-first AI tutor that walks K-12 students through Indian and regional history in conversational Bengali, adapting its explanations and pacing to how each student is progressing.

Stack: LLM-based tutoring engine, ASP.NET Core, Azure
View on GitHub

ReturnGuard AI

In development

Retailers selling across multiple channels struggle to catch fraudulent or abusive return patterns before they eat into margin — by the time it's obvious in the numbers, it's already cost money.

ReturnGuard AI is a copilot that watches return activity across channels and flags patterns that look like fraud or abuse, combining rule-based checks with ML scoring so a human reviewer can decide fast.

Stack: .NET backend, Azure, rules engine, ML scoring
View on GitHub

LexVault

In development

Legal teams need to search and reason over large volumes of case documents without losing precision on exact clauses or missing the broader context around them.

LexVault retrieves legal documents through a dual-pipeline design: one pipeline optimized for precise clause lookup, and a second for broader contextual reasoning across a case file.

Stack: RAG pipeline, ASP.NET Core, Azure, vector search
View on GitHub

InboxPilot

In development

Support and sales inboxes drown teams in email that needs to be triaged, tagged, and routed to the right person before anyone can actually act on it.

InboxPilot is a multi-tenant SaaS product that triages incoming email automatically. It started as a proposal for a single client and grew into a product of its own.

Stack: ASP.NET Core, EF Core, Docker Compose
View on GitHub
